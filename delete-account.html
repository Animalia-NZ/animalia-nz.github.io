Delete Your Animalia NZ Account

We're sorry to see you go! You can delete your Animalia NZ account directly from the mobile app.

How to Delete Your Account

Step 1: Open the Animalia NZ app on your mobile device
Step 2: Navigate to your Profile screen (tap the profile icon in the bottom navigation)
Step 3: Scroll down and tap the "Delete Account" button
Step 4: Confirm your choice when prompted

⚠️ Important Information

What gets deleted:

  • Your account and profile information
  • All animal records and data you've created
  • Photos and images you've uploaded
  • Breeding records and history
  • All personal data associated with your account

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Need Help?

If you're unable to delete your account through the app, or if you have any questions, please contact us:

Email: support@animalia.nz

We will process your account deletion request within 30 days.

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you prefer, you can also request account deletion by emailing us at support@animalia.nz with:

  • Your registered email address
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
